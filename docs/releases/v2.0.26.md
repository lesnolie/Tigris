# Tigris v2.0.26

## 中文

- **应用图标底板统一：** 桌面所有 App 磁贴改用同一块 `#E1E5EA` 雾面浅灰底板，顶部略亮、底部略暗的轻微渐变，11dp 圆角，1dp 淡灰细边，柔和低透明度外阴影。不再按 App 主色染底，也不再混用 16:9 banner。
- **图标保持原样：** 第三方 App 的图标继续使用安装包里的原图，保持原色、原比例和原内部背景，只按原有 64% 视觉比例居中显示；不重绘、不裁切、不羽化。Kodi、Emotn、ES 文件管理器、当贝等均未改动图标资源。
- **内置页面：** 设置页改用设置页自己的齿轮图标（有用户自定义图标时优先使用自定义图标）；壁纸页保留蓝紫渐变图标。
- **焦点状态：** 选中磁贴仍使用同一灰底，增加白色细环和浅蓝光晕，并保留轻微上浮；遥控器导航、点击、长按和应用排列逻辑不变。
- **多壁纸可读性：** 底板不透明，浅色壁纸下由细边和阴影分离边界，深色、照片、动态和高对比度壁纸下由浅灰底板本身保持辨识度。
- **同时包含 v2.0.25 的投屏修复：** MiracastReceiver v1.2.14 更换包名后，更新中心按新包名 `top.jast.miracastreceiver` 检查、安装和打开；旧包名仍存在时提供「卸载旧版」。已在 Sony XR-55A80J 上验证。
- Android 9+，versionCode **33**；Standard / Guard 使用原签名覆盖升级，保留数据。本次只改桌面 App 磁贴的外观，不修改图标资源、应用排列、启动、长按或更新逻辑；文件夹和 HDMI 输入源容器保持原样式。

- Standard: `Tigris-v2.0.26.apk`
- Guard: `Tigris-guard-v2.0.26.apk`
- SHA-256: `SHA256SUMS-v2.0.26.txt`

## English

- **One tile surface:** every home-screen app tile now uses the same `#E1E5EA` frost-grey card, with a barely-there top-light/bottom-dark gradient, 11 dp corners, a 1 dp grey hairline and a soft low-opacity outer shadow. Per-app colour tinting and 16:9 banner rendering are gone.
- **Icons untouched:** third-party icons still come from the installed APK at their original colour, proportions and internal background, centred at the established 64% optical size. Nothing is redrawn, cropped or feathered; Kodi, Emotn, ES File Explorer, 当贝 and the rest keep their own artwork.
- **Built-in screens:** Settings uses its own gear (a user-uploaded custom icon still wins), and the wallpaper screen keeps its blue-violet icon.
- **Focus:** the selected tile keeps the same grey surface, adds a white ring and a soft light-blue halo, and keeps the slight lift. D-pad navigation, click, long-press and app ordering are unchanged.
- **Wallpaper readability:** the surface is opaque. On light wallpapers the hairline and shadow define the edge; on dark, photo, video and high-contrast wallpapers the light grey card itself does the work.
- **Includes the v2.0.25 casting fix:** after MiracastReceiver v1.2.14 renamed its package, the update center checks, installs and opens `top.jast.miracastreceiver`; a pre-rename install offers **Uninstall old** first. Verified on a Sony XR-55A80J.
- Android 9+, versionCode **33**; matching Standard/Guard upgrades keep the existing signing identity and user data. This release only changes the appearance of home-screen app tiles; icon resources, app ordering, launch, long-press and update behaviour are unchanged. Folder and HDMI input containers keep their existing style.

- Standard: `Tigris-v2.0.26.apk`
- Guard: `Tigris-guard-v2.0.26.apk`
- SHA-256: `SHA256SUMS-v2.0.26.txt`
